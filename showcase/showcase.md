# Kessler

The sky is full of yesterday’s spacecraft. You have one demolition ball, a deflector, and a planet worth protecting. Circle the world and turn every returning shot into another chance to clear its orbit.

Three rings of derelicts surround you. The inner ring holds still, armored wrecks drift clockwise, and the outer belt sweeps against them. Catch the ball near the edge of your paddle to send it through a gap, carve a new route, or finish a damaged target.

Every broken hull can shed salvage. Catch expansion braces, launch extra balls, raise a shield, or punch straight through armor. Watch the red pods: they shrink the paddle just when you need more room.

Sweep the last wreck away and take a breath. The next wave comes faster. Keep the ball away from the planet, protect your three lives, and see how long you can keep the sky clear.
